Run your mess, simplified.

This page covers common questions about the KhaneMe Business app, for mess and tiffin service owners. If you can't find what you need below, write to us directly.

Common questions

A few things owners usually ask about first.

I can't log in or sign up

Make sure you're on the latest version of the app from the App Store, then try again. If it persists, email us with the phone number or email tied to your business account.

My mess timings or menu won't save

Double-check the timing entries don't overlap, then try saving again. If it still fails, send us a screenshot of what you entered.

My menu images aren't uploading

Check your internet connection and that the image isn't too large. If the upload keeps failing, email us the image and your account details.

The app crashed or won't load

Force-close and reopen the app, and check the App Store for an update. If it keeps happening, let us know your device model and iOS version.

I want to delete my account or data

Email us from your registered address asking for account deletion. We'll confirm and remove your business account data.

Something else

Not covered here? Just write in — describe what you were doing and what happened.

Still need help?

We typically reply within 1–2 business days.
